About Lonavala

Lonavala is a beautiful hill station located in the Sahyadri mountain range of Maharashtra. It is a popular destination for nature lovers, families, friends, couples and trekking enthusiasts. Lonavala is famous for its scenic valleys, waterfalls, forts, caves, lakes and pleasant weather.

How to Reach Lonavala

🚆 By Train

Lonavala railway station is connected with Pune and Mumbai.

🚗 By Road

Lonavala is easily accessible by road from Pune and Mumbai.

✈️ By Air

Pune Airport is the nearest major airport option.
