About Mahabaleshwar

Mahabaleshwar is a popular hill station in the Western Ghats of Maharashtra. It is known for its beautiful valleys, viewpoints, waterfalls, forests and pleasant climate. The destination is also famous for strawberries and scenic landscapes.

How to Reach Mahabaleshwar

🚗 By Road

Mahabaleshwar is well connected by road with Pune, Mumbai and nearby cities.

🚌 By Bus

State transport and private buses are available from major nearby cities.

✈️ By Air

Pune Airport is a major airport option for reaching Mahabaleshwar.
